# UMart

UMart connects people around a community's listed goods, services, and errands. These terms describe the shared request and transaction model.

## Language

**UMart Member**:
A person with a UMart account who can participate in a community. Membership does not verify that the person lives in that community.
_Avoid_: Verified resident

**Buyer**:
A UMart Member who submits an Order Request for a Listing.
_Avoid_: Customer, consumer

**Seller**:
A UMart Member who offers a Marketplace item or food and drink through a Listing.

**Provider**:
A UMart Member who offers a service or errand through a Listing.

**Listing**:
A structured offer of an item, food or drink, service, or errand authored by a UMart Member. A Listing may be a draft, held for review, live, rejected, or removed.

**Live Listing**:
A published Listing that people with the link can browse. It may be unavailable for new requests because it is paused, not yet open, or sold out.

**Held Listing**:
A Listing awaiting founder/admin review before publication.

**Food Batch**:
A dated quantity of food or drink offered through a reusable Listing for a particular fulfillment window.

**Service Slot**:
A scheduled time window with limited capacity under a reusable service Listing.

**Errand Trip**:
A scheduled journey with limited capacity under a reusable errand Listing.

**Order Request**:
A Buyer's proposal to obtain the item, food or drink, service, or errand described in a Listing. The Seller or Provider has not yet confirmed its terms.

**Expired Order Request**:
An unconfirmed Order Request whose response or discussion deadline has passed.

**Transaction**:
The agreed arrangement between a Buyer and a Seller or Provider created when the Seller or Provider confirms an Order Request, including final scope, price, timing, and handoff.

**Awaiting buyer confirmation**:
A Transaction whose Seller or Provider has claimed fulfillment but whose Buyer has not yet confirmed or disputed that claim.

**Buyer-confirmed completion**:
A seller's or provider's claim of fulfillment that the buyer explicitly confirms.

**Auto-confirmed completion**:
A seller's or provider's claim of fulfillment that closes after the buyer makes no response within the allowed time.

**Moderator-verified completion**:
A seller's or provider's claim of fulfillment that a moderator upholds after the buyer disputes it, without buyer confirmation or public completion credit.

**Disputed Transaction**:
A Transaction whose claimed fulfillment the buyer contests and a moderator has not resolved.
