# 04: Publish a Marketplace item

Status: ready-for-agent
Blocked by: 03 UMart Member sign-in and profiles

## What to build

A Seller creates and edits a one-off Marketplace item Listing. The author sees the publication state, and guests can discover the Listing once it is live.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] The Seller can enter title, description, price, condition, stock, handoff method, and photos, and can keep a draft or publish a Live Listing.
- [ ] Public Listing rules and the warning that text and photos are visible to anyone with the link appear before posting. The rules recommend private sharing of exact room and contact details and forbid another person's private information.
- [ ] Listing photos are resized and compressed before storage. The Seller can edit their own Listing; another Member cannot edit it.
- [ ] Publication state is distinct from availability, and a guest sees only public fields of a Live Listing.
- [ ] A running-app test covers authoring, guest discovery, and denied cross-account edits.
