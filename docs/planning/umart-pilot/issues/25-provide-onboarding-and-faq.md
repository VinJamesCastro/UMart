# 25: Provide onboarding and FAQ

Status: ready-for-agent
Blocked by: 03 UMart Member sign-in and profiles

## What to build

A new Member sees brief onboarding and can revisit practical UMart guidance in Help/FAQ. The guidance explains the confirmed pilot rules in plain language.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Onboarding describes UMart as a UHome Community Marketplace without residency verification and introduces the four categories and UMart Member identity.
- [ ] It explains that Listing text and photos are public to anyone with the link, optional floors require sign-in, no room is collected at signup, and exact contact or meeting details are better shared privately.
- [ ] It explains Order Requests, Seller or Provider confirmation, direct payment and handoff, fulfillment claims, Buyer confirmation, community rules, and reporting.
- [ ] Help/FAQ covers availability, pre-orders, deadlines, expiry and erasure, saved Listings, blocking, ratings, support, food-safety responsibility, and web-push limits. iOS guidance explains Home Screen installation before push permission.
- [ ] A Member can revisit Help after onboarding. Tests check the main entry points and content against the pilot's actual behavior.
