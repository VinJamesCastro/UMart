# 05: Review keyword-held Listings

Status: ready-for-agent
Blocked by: 04 Publish a Marketplace item

## What to build

A prohibited keyword exact match holds a Listing for founder/admin review. The founder can approve a false positive, ask for edits, or reject it, while ordinary Listings publish without a manual wait.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Every exact match on the founder-approved prohibited keyword list creates a Held Listing; the match never rejects it automatically.
- [ ] The founder/admin has a protected review view and can approve, reject, or request edits. Only approval makes a Held Listing guest-visible.
- [ ] The author sees publication status and reason and can edit and resubmit when appropriate.
- [ ] Published rules prohibit stolen or counterfeit goods, weapons, controlled substances, alcohol, tobacco, vapes, gambling, sexual services, identity-document or account sales, harassment, spam, threats, scams, misleading details, impersonation, and disclosure of another person's private information. They explain that UMart does not verify food safety.
- [ ] Tests cover an ordinary Listing, a false positive, a rejected Listing, and denial of review actions to a regular Member.
