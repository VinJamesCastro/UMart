# 08: Save Listings privately

Status: ready-for-agent
Blocked by: 03 UMart Member sign-in and profiles, 07 Browse Home and Explore

## What to build

A Member privately saves and unsaves Listings from discovery or detail views and can return to those bookmarks in Profile.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Saving requires sign-in and creates a bookmark visible only to its owner.
- [ ] A saved Listing remains identifiable as unavailable or removed until the Member unsaves it, without exposing removed content beyond what identification needs.
- [ ] Saving does not send price or availability alerts.
- [ ] Tests cover save, unsave, unavailable and removed states, and denied cross-account reads.
