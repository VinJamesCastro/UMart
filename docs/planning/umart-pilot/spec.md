# Build the UHome UMart pilot

## Problem Statement

People around UHome currently have to find offers scattered through group-chat posts, ask whether they are still available, and move to personal accounts to coordinate. Sellers and Providers repeat posts and cannot show current stock, schedules, or a reliable record of a proposed deal. Buyers cannot easily distinguish an offer from a confirmed Transaction. The pilot needs to learn whether a structured community marketplace gets people through discovery, discussion, and Buyer-confirmed completion, and whether Buyers return.

## Solution

Launch UMart as a mobile-friendly UHome Community Marketplace shared through the UHome group chat. Anyone with the link can browse Live Listings and their public fields. Google sign-in creates a UMart Member account for posting, private profiles, saved Listings, messaging, Order Requests, reports, and ratings. Membership does not verify residency.

All four categories launch: Marketplace items, Food & Drinks, Services, and Errands / Pasabuy. Listings show current availability and let a Buyer submit one Order Request for one Listing. The Seller or Provider discusses terms, then confirms a Transaction and commits stock or capacity. The parties arrange payment and fulfillment directly. The Seller or Provider claims fulfillment; the Buyer can confirm or dispute it. Only Buyer-confirmed completion earns public credit or ratings. The founder handles moderation and support through a protected admin view.

## User Stories

1. As a guest, I want to open the shared UMart link on my phone, so that I can see offers around UHome without signing in.
2. As a guest, I want to browse titles, descriptions, photos, prices or rates, categories, availability, and public usernames on Live Listings, so that I can decide whether to join.
3. As a guest, I want unavailable Live Listings to show their state, so that I do not mistake an old offer for one I can request now.
4. As a guest, I want a clear sign-in path when I try a member action, so that I know how to participate.
5. As a guest, I want UMart described as a UHome Community Marketplace, so that I am not led to believe its accounts prove residency.
6. As a UMart Member, I want to sign in with Google and choose a public username, so that I can participate without showing my Google identity to other members.
7. As a UMart Member, I want to share an optional, self-reported UHome floor, so that other signed-in members can see it when I choose.
8. As a UMart Member, I want no signup room field, so that I can keep exact meeting details private until I decide to share them.
9. As a UMart Member, I want one account for buying, selling, and providing services or errands, so that my activity stays together.
10. As a UMart Member, I want my full profile visible only to signed-in members, so that casual guests see only the public identity on Listings.
11. As a UMart Member, I want a profile for my description, Listings, and separate buyer and seller/provider reputation, so that others can assess my marketplace activity.
12. As a UMart Member, I want my buying and selling history to stay private to the participants and a moderator handling a case, so that strangers cannot inspect my deals.
13. As a Seller, I want to create a Marketplace Listing with photos, condition, price, stock, availability, and handoff method, so that Buyers understand the item.
14. As a Seller, I want a one-off Marketplace item's stock tracked on its Listing, so that its remaining quantity is clear.
15. As a Seller, I want a reusable Food & Drinks Listing with dated Food Batches, so that each fulfillment window has its own quantity.
16. As a Provider, I want a reusable service Listing with dated Service Slots, so that Buyers can request a time with remaining capacity.
17. As a Provider, I want a reusable errand Listing with dated Errand Trips, destination, departure and expected return, fee, purchase scope, and capacity, so that Buyers know what I can do.
18. As a Seller or Provider, I want recurring, specific-date, one-time, and until-sold-out availability where applicable, so that my Listing reflects when I can fulfill it.
19. As a Seller or Provider, I want to pause an offering, so that it stops taking requests without losing its Live Listing.
20. As a Seller or Provider, I want draft, held, live, rejected, and removed publication states separate from availability, so that review and stock do not get confused.
21. As a Seller, I want to enable pre-ordering on a food or drink Listing only when useful, so that ready-to-order offerings remain simple.
22. As a Seller, I want to set a Food Batch quantity, pre-order opening, cutoff, fulfillment window, and response deadline, so that Buyers know when to request and when to expect an answer.
23. As a Buyer, I want to request an open pre-order before its cutoff, so that I can ask for a future Food Batch without assuming it is reserved.
24. As a Buyer, I want to see a pre-order's response deadline before submitting, so that I know when the Seller must respond.
25. As a visitor, I want Home to mix Live Listings from all four categories, so that I can discover offers without choosing a category first.
26. As a visitor, I want visible shortcuts to Food & Drinks, Marketplace, Services, and Errands / Pasabuy, so that I can open the right Explore view directly.
27. As a visitor, I want Available Now, Pre-order Open, and unavailable states shown accurately, so that I can tell what action is possible.
28. As a visitor, I want search across all categories or within one category, so that I can find a particular offer.
29. As a visitor, I want Food & Drinks filters for type, availability or pre-order, and price, so that I can narrow food offers.
30. As a visitor, I want Marketplace filters for item type, condition, and price, so that I can narrow physical items.
31. As a visitor, I want Services filters for type, availability, and price, so that I can find a suitable Service Slot.
32. As a visitor, I want applicable newest and price sorting, so that I can order results usefully.
33. As a visitor, I want each empty category to invite the first real Listing, so that an empty launch does not look broken.
34. As a UMart Member, I want to save a Listing privately, so that I can return to it later.
35. As a UMart Member, I want a saved Listing to remain identifiable when unavailable or removed until I unsave it, so that my bookmark does not silently disappear.
36. As a Buyer, I want to message a Seller or Provider from a Listing, so that we can discuss an offer without sharing social accounts.
37. As a Buyer, I want to submit an Order Request for one Listing and a requested quantity or slot, proposed time, and handoff, so that the other party can consider a concrete proposal.
38. As a Buyer, I want the offered title and price or rate copied into my Order Request, so that later Listing edits cannot silently change my proposal.
39. As a Buyer, I want to know that submitting an Order Request does not reserve stock or guarantee fulfillment, so that I do not treat it as a confirmed purchase or booking.
40. As a Seller or Provider, I want a notice for each new Order Request and its response deadline, so that I can answer on time.
41. As a Seller or Provider, I want to mark a Pending Request Discussing, so that I can acknowledge it while we settle terms.
42. As either participant, I want to message within an Order Request, so that we can agree on details before confirmation.
43. As a Seller or Provider, I want to decline each request I cannot fulfill, so that the Buyer receives a clear answer.
44. As either participant, I want to cancel an unconfirmed request with a reason and notice, so that the other party knows it ended.
45. As either participant, I want the applicable response or discussion deadline visible, so that I know when an unanswered request will expire.
46. As either participant, I want an Expired Order Request hidden from normal Orders immediately, so that it is not mistaken for an active proposal.
47. As a UMart Member, I want an Expired Order Request and its request-only messages and notices erased after seven days, so that abandoned personal details are not kept indefinitely.
48. As a reporter, I want evidence for an open moderation case retained only until resolution, so that the founder can review it before the request evidence is erased.
49. As a Seller or Provider, I want to choose which pending requests to confirm, so that submission order does not decide whom I can fulfill.
50. As a Seller or Provider, I want to confirm final quantity or scope, price or fee, schedule, and handoff, so that the Order Request becomes a clear Transaction.
51. As a Buyer, I want notice and a private record of confirmed terms, so that I know the Seller or Provider committed to them.
52. As either participant, I want confirmed terms fixed, so that a correction requires cancellation and a new Order Request rather than an unseen edit.
53. As a Buyer, I want a sold-out one-off Listing to stop accepting new requests after its last unit is committed, so that I cannot ask for already committed stock.
54. As a Seller or Provider, I want only genuinely usable cancelled stock or capacity restored, so that elapsed slots, trips, or batches are not offered again.
55. As a Seller, I want to mark a Confirmed physical item or food order ready for pickup, so that the Buyer gets a fulfillment update without counting it as completion.
56. As a Seller or Provider, I want to claim fulfillment of a Confirmed Transaction, so that the Buyer can respond to that claim.
57. As a Buyer, I want to confirm a fulfillment claim, so that the Transaction becomes a Buyer-confirmed completion.
58. As a Buyer, I want to dispute a fulfillment claim, so that the founder can review what happened.
59. As a Buyer, I want seven days after Auto-confirmed completion to confirm or dispute in the Transaction, so that a missed initial response does not immediately close that route.
60. As either participant, I want a Confirmed Transaction cancellable with a reason and notice, so that a deal that cannot proceed has a recorded end.
61. As either participant, I want private buying or selling history with status, other party, and agreed terms, so that I can follow active and past Transactions.
62. As a UMart Member, I want public completion counts based only on Buyer-confirmed completions, so that reputation reflects an explicit Buyer response.
63. As a Buyer, I want to rate and review the Seller or Provider once after Buyer-confirmed completion, so that I can share my experience.
64. As a Seller or Provider, I want to rate and review the Buyer once after Buyer-confirmed completion, so that their conduct has its own reputation record.
65. As a UMart Member, I want buyer and seller/provider rating averages and counts shown separately, so that one role does not hide the other.
66. As a UMart Member, I want public reviews to omit private deal, contact, room, and conversation details, so that reviews do not expose personal information.
67. As a UMart Member, I want to report a Listing, Member, Order Request, Transaction, or conversation with context and evidence, so that the founder can assess problematic conduct.
68. As a UMart Member, I want to block another Member, so that they cannot send me new direct messages or Order Requests and do not appear in my signed-in discovery.
69. As a UMart Member, I want an existing Confirmed Transaction with a blocked Member still visible with cancel and report actions, so that I can resolve it without new direct contact.
70. As a Seller or Provider, I want the Listing rules visible before posting, so that I know which offers are prohibited and how public my content will be.
71. As a Seller or Provider, I want a prohibited keyword exact match to hold my Listing for review rather than reject it automatically, so that a false positive can be approved.
72. As a Seller or Provider, I want to see a held, rejected, or removed Listing's status and reason and edit and resubmit when appropriate, so that I can correct it.
73. As a founder/admin, I want a protected queue for Held Listings, reports, suspicious patterns, disputes, and support requests, so that I can review evidence and act.
74. As a founder/admin, I want to approve, reject, request edits, or remove Listings and warn, restrict, or suspend Members, so that I can apply the published rules.
75. As a founder/admin, I want to resolve a Disputed Transaction by upholding or removing the fulfillment claim and record the action, so that the Transaction reflects the review.
76. As a UMart Member, I want in-app notices for marketplace, messaging, Transaction, and support events, so that I can catch updates when push is unavailable.
77. As a UMart Member, I want optional web push for new requests, confirmations, cancellations, and messages, so that I can see time-sensitive updates when my browser supports it.
78. As a UMart Member, I want push text to avoid message bodies and private deal details, so that a lock-screen notice does not expose them.
79. As a new UMart Member, I want brief onboarding about public Listings, optional floors, requests, direct payment, and safety rules, so that I understand the pilot before participating.
80. As a UMart Member, I want a Help/FAQ area I can revisit, so that I can understand deadlines, expiry, privacy, pre-orders, ratings, reporting, and push limits.
81. As a UMart Member, I want to submit a support issue with category, description, related record, and optional evidence, so that the founder can investigate a problem.
82. As a founder/admin, I want support status and updates recorded, so that I can follow an issue through resolution.
83. As a founder/admin, I want counts of accounts, Listings, Order Requests, and Buyer-confirmed Transactions overall and by category, so that I can assess pilot use.
84. As a founder/admin, I want first-four-week pilot targets to exclude founder/test activity and self-transactions, so that reported success reflects actual community use.
85. As a visitor, I want to discover new, popular, or active Live Listings, so that useful offers do not depend on a single search query.

## Implementation Decisions

- Build the UHome pilot as a mobile-friendly website distributed through a UHome group-chat link. Use Cloudflare Pages on its provided address and Supabase Free for Google authentication, application data, and storage, subject to provider limits checked before deployment. The founder operates support and moderation through a protected admin view. Members pay no UMart fee during the pilot.
- Model one initial community, UHome, without coupling the account model to verified residency. Support the 38 UHome floor choices as optional self-reported profile data. Do not collect a room number at signup or in the pilot profile. Use the terms UMart Member, Buyer, Seller, Provider, Listing, Order Request, and Transaction from the domain context.
- A Google account identifies the Member privately. Public identity is the chosen username and UMart Member label. Guests can read Live Listing public fields. Full profiles, optional floors, saved Listings, messages, requests, posting, ratings, and reports require sign-in. Enforce each restriction in Supabase authorization and storage policies as well as the interface.
- A Listing has a publication state independent of request availability. Publication states are draft, held for review, live, rejected, and removed. A Live Listing remains browseable while paused, not yet open, sold out, or otherwise unavailable, with its state and request action shown correctly. Only Live Listings appear in guest discovery. Removed Listings remain identifiable to Members who saved them until unsaved without exposing removed content beyond what that behavior needs.
- Provide shared Listing fields for public title, description, photos, price or rate, category, availability, author username, and handoff options. Category-specific fields cover Marketplace condition and Listing stock; Food Batch quantity and fulfillment window; Service Slot schedule and capacity; and Errand Trip destination, departure, expected return, purchase scope, fee, and capacity. Keep reusable Food, Service, and Errand Listings separate from their dated batches, slots, and trips.
- Evaluate and display schedules, deadlines, and fulfillment windows in Philippine time, Asia/Manila. Support recurring, specific-date, one-time, and until-sold-out availability where applicable. A Seller or Provider can pause an offering. Available Now requires an open availability window, remaining stock or capacity where applicable, and no pause. Pre-order Open requires an open food pre-order request window. Other Live Listings show an unavailable state and cannot take a new request.
- Food pre-ordering is optional per Seller. A pre-order window specifies opening, request cutoff, fulfillment window, Food Batch quantity, and a Seller-set response deadline after cutoff and no later than fulfillment start. Accept requests only during the open window. Display the response deadline before submission. A request does not reserve quantity, and the Seller can confirm pending requests in any order.
- The five primary areas are Home, Explore, Orders, Inbox, and Profile. Home combines Live Listings from all four categories with visible category shortcuts and discovery of new, popular, or active offers. Explore supports search across all categories or within one. Food & Drinks has type, available-now or pre-order, and price filters plus newest or price sorting. Marketplace has item type, condition, and price filters plus newest or price sorting. Services has type, availability, and price filters plus newest or price sorting. Errands / Pasabuy needs no initial subcategory filter or category-specific sort. Empty categories invite the first Listing. Orders holds private buying and selling history; Inbox holds conversations; Profile holds member identity, Listings, reputation, private saved Listings, history, and Help access.
- Saved Listings are private bookmarks. Saving does not subscribe a Member to price or availability alerts. A saved Listing remains identifiable when unavailable or removed until the Member unsaves it.
- One Order Request refers to one Listing and may request multiple units. On submission, snapshot Listing title, offered price or rate, quantity or slot, proposed time, handoff, and both participants. Later Listing edits do not change it. Submission sends the Seller or Provider an in-app notice, shows both parties the applicable deadline, and commits no stock or capacity.
- An ordinary Pending Request expires at the earlier of 24 hours after submission or proposed fulfillment start unless the Seller or Provider declines, confirms, or marks it Discussing. Discussing satisfies the first deadline, then expires at fulfillment start or, if no start exists, after seven days without a message. A food pre-order uses its Seller-set response deadline. Either party can cancel an unconfirmed Order Request with a reason and notice; the Seller or Provider declines each unfillable request manually.
- Hide an Expired Order Request from normal Orders immediately at its deadline. Erase it, its request-only messages, and its notices seven days after expiry. Keep only evidence needed by an open report or moderation case; erase that request evidence when the case closes and retain minimal outcome and account-action history. A separate Listing conversation may remain. This follows the expired-request ADR.
- Confirmation is one authoritative operation that checks the applicable Listing stock or Food Batch, Service Slot, or Errand Trip capacity, commits the count, freezes the agreed Transaction scope, price or fee, schedule, and handoff, and sends the Buyer an in-app notice. Concurrent confirmations cannot claim the same last unit. A separate Buyer acceptance step is not required. Correct confirmed terms by cancelling and submitting a new Order Request. Pending requests for a newly sold-out one-off Listing remain visible to their Buyers until individually declined or expired.
- On cancellation of a Confirmed Transaction, the Seller or Provider chooses how much committed count is genuinely usable again, no more than the cancelled amount. Do not restore capacity for an elapsed Service Slot or Errand Trip or an expired Food Batch. Completion does not restore capacity. Apply the same rule when moderation cancels a false claim.
- Transaction states cover Confirmed, Awaiting buyer confirmation, Buyer-confirmed completion, Auto-confirmed completion, Disputed, Moderator-verified completion, and Cancelled. A Seller or Provider may mark an item ready for pickup without claiming completion. A fulfillment claim starts the Buyer's 72-hour response window. Buyer confirmation earns public and pilot credit. No response produces Auto-confirmed completion, followed by seven days in which the Buyer can still confirm or dispute within the Transaction; later objections use reporting. A moderator upholding a dispute produces Moderator-verified completion. A false claim returns to Confirmed if both parties continue or Cancelled otherwise. Record moderation action.
- Only Buyer-confirmed completion unlocks one optional rating and review from each participant and counts publicly. A later explicit Buyer confirmation can change an eligible Auto-confirmed or Moderator-verified completion into a Buyer-confirmed completion. Show buyer and seller/provider rating averages and counts separately. Reviews identify the author's role and omit private order, contact, room, and message details. Moderators can remove abusive reviews or those tied to fabricated Transactions. Auto-confirmed and Moderator-verified completions remain separately visible to the participants and in operator counts without public credit while unconfirmed by the Buyer, as the completion ADR requires.
- Provide Listing-level and Order Request-linked conversations. Only participants can read their private conversation. Pre-confirmation discussion does not mutate snapshots automatically; confirmed terms are fixed. Blocking stops new direct messages and Order Requests in both directions and hides the blocked Member from the blocker's signed-in views. An existing Confirmed Transaction remains visible to its participants with cancel and report actions, but no new direct contact. Guest-visible Live Listings can still be seen without a signed-in block filter.
- Publish the Listing rules before posting. Require accurate offer, price or rate, quantity or capacity, availability, handoff details, and honest photos. Prohibit stolen or counterfeit goods; weapons or dangerous items; controlled substances or illegal drugs; alcohol, tobacco, or vapes; gambling; sexual services; account, ID, or identity-document sales; harassment, spam, threats, scams, impersonation, and disclosure of another person's private information. Explain that UMart does not verify food safety or kitchen conditions. Normal Listings can go live immediately. Every exact match against the founder-approved prohibited keyword list holds a Listing for review, never automatic rejection. Reports, repeated reports, and less certain warning signs enter a review queue without automatically hiding a Live Listing. The founder/admin can approve a false positive, request edits, reject, remove, warn, restrict, or suspend as the rules warrant. Show authors status and reason and allow edit and resubmit when appropriate.
- Reports can target a Member, Listing, Order Request, Transaction, message, or conversation and include reason, written context, related records, and private photo or screenshot evidence. Resize and compress public Listing photos before upload; keep report and support evidence private in storage. Store report status and action history. A report is an allegation for review, not proof. Restrict report evidence to the reporter and authorized moderator as appropriate; the moderator can access private Transaction details only while handling a report or support issue.
- Keep support issues separate from conduct reports. Support captures category, summary, description, optional related record, and private evidence, with Open, Under review, Resolved, and Closed status. Categories cover technical, account/profile, Listing, Order Request or Transaction, messaging, safety, payment-related, and other issues. Payment concerns may lead to conduct review, but UMart does not adjudicate debts or issue refunds.
- In-app notices cover request, message, Transaction, review, Listing, support, and report events where appropriate. Offer best-effort web push for new requests, Seller or Provider confirmations, cancellations, and new messages. Push requires permission and compatible browser behavior; iOS instructions must explain Home Screen installation before requesting permission. Push may use a public Listing title and sender username but strips phone numbers and exact room patterns and excludes message bodies, prices, and private deal details. In-app deadlines remain visible without push.
- Onboarding and Help/FAQ explain public Listing exposure, unverified membership, optional floor, private meeting details, all four categories, request and confirmation flow, expiry and erasure, direct payment and fulfillment, ratings, rules, reporting, and push limits. Listing text and photos are public to anyone with the link. A Seller may voluntarily include their own contact or room details, but Listings may not reveal another person's private information.
- Payments and physical handoffs happen directly between Buyer and Seller or Provider. UMart records the arrangement and state but does not handle money or dispatch. The parties can agree on pickup, Seller delivery, or fulfillment through an Errand Trip.
- Count the first four weeks from sharing the UHome group-chat link. Report accounts, Listings, Order Requests, and Buyer-confirmed Transactions overall and by category. Preserve non-identifying aggregate Order Request counts when individual Expired Order Requests are erased; do not retain request evidence or identifiers for metrics. The pilot targets at least 20 Buyer-confirmed Transactions, 10 distinct Buyers with one, and 5 Buyers with a later Buyer-confirmed Transaction. Exclude founder/test activity and self-transactions from target counts; keep Auto-confirmed and Moderator-verified outcomes separate. Do not call accounts verified tenants.

## Testing Decisions

- Test observable behavior at the highest available seam: a running pilot web app against an isolated Supabase environment. Drive guest, Member, Buyer, Seller or Provider, and founder/admin actions through public application flows and verify the resulting visible state and access with the same credentials. Use controlled time and seeded records for deadlines. A good test asserts what each actor can do or see, not component structure, table layout, or helper calls.
- There is no application code or test suite in the current repository, so there is no test prior art to copy and no existing feature seam to preserve. Establish this one end-to-end pilot seam first. Add focused direct authorization probes against Supabase policies for private records and storage because browser rendering alone cannot prove data isolation.
- Cover guest visibility, Google-authenticated participation, optional floor access, public username separation from Google identity, unauthorized reads and writes, and private report evidence across separate Member accounts. Check both interface results and denied direct data access.
- Exercise all four Listing types, publication and availability as separate states, Asia/Manila boundary times, pause, stock or capacity, Available Now, Pre-order Open, empty categories, search, filters, sorting, and saved Listing behavior.
- Exercise Order Request snapshots and no reservation, ordinary and pre-order deadlines, Discussing, decline, cancellation, expiry hiding, seven-day erasure, case evidence holds and release, and separate Listing conversations. Assert the public and private views before and after time advances.
- Submit competing confirmations for the final unit or slot and assert that exactly one Transaction commits it. Check that confirmed terms stay fixed, sold-out Listings reject new requests, other pending requests remain, and cancellation restores only usable capacity.
- Exercise fulfillment claim, ready-for-pickup update, Buyer confirmation, 72-hour Auto-confirmed completion, the later seven-day Buyer response, dispute, moderator resolution, and false-claim correction. Verify only Buyer-confirmed completion changes public counts or unlocks reviews.
- Verify one review per participant per Buyer-confirmed Transaction, separate buyer and seller/provider summaries, review privacy, blocking, published rules, exact-match holds, false-positive approval, reports, support, and moderator access controls.
- Check that in-app notices and deadlines work without push. Where push is supported, inspect payload content for forbidden private details and test permission or delivery failure without losing in-app state. Run a mobile viewport smoke check through discovery, request, discussion, and confirmation.
- Verify pilot reporting with seeded founder/test accounts, self-transactions, repeated Buyers, and all completion types. Reconcile the displayed totals with the underlying Buyer-confirmed Transactions and the first-four-week window. Check that Order Request aggregate counts survive expiry erasure without retaining Buyer, Seller or Provider, message, or request identifiers.

## Out of Scope

- Payment processing, wallet, escrow, refunds, or payment-dispute adjudication.
- Internal delivery fleet, rider accounts, live rider tracking, dispatch, or route optimization.
- Official UHome administration integration or verified-resident claims and partnerships.
- AI recommendations or assistant, advanced seller analytics, and a general-purpose social network.
- Price or availability alerts for saved Listings and a complex notification-preferences system.
- Multi-community launch or advanced community-management infrastructure beyond the UHome pilot.

## Further Notes

- This spec synthesizes the confirmed pilot in the product requirements and uses the domain context and the three ADRs as binding decisions. It is a large pilot build; implementation can be split into independently verifiable issues without changing these behaviors.
- The testing seam is a proposal for this new codebase. It keeps the external pilot journey together and adds direct policy checks for data that must remain private.
- Initial hosting at about ₱0 is an operating target within free-service limits, not a guarantee. Check current Cloudflare Pages, Supabase, storage, and push limits before deployment. Monitor quotas and keep recoverable copies of essential data.
- The first-four-week success targets are measurement criteria for the pilot, not a gate requiring a completed Transaction in every category.
